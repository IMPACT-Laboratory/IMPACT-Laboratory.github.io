Zach Ayers is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering at the University of South Florida. He joined USF in 2026 after serving as a Principal Research Engineer at the RTX Technology Research Center where he collaborated with the Pratt & Whitney and Raytheon business units to develop rotating detonation engine technology for high-speed flight. His industry experience has given him a unique understanding of the challenges that need to be addressed in high-speed flight.

Dr. Ayers received his PhD and MS in Mechanical Engineering from Purdue University in 2023, after completing his BS in Mechanical Engineering at the University of Alabama. He has a strong background in experimental combustion testing, flight system analysis, thermodynamic modeling, and laser diagnostic measurements for reacting flows.
